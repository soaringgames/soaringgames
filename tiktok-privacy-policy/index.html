Privacy Policy

Soaring Games Uploader · last updated 29 September 2026

Who this covers

Soaring Games Uploader is software operated by Soaring Games LLC to publish Soaring Games' own short-form videos to Soaring Games' own social accounts, including TikTok. It is not a consumer product, has no user accounts, and is not offered to the public.

What we collect

Nothing from you. The software is used only by Soaring Games LLC and collects no information from visitors to this website or from viewers of our videos.

When Soaring Games LLC connects its own TikTok account, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token. Those tokens, and the TikTok account identifier they belong to, are stored on a single private computer owned by Soaring Games LLC. They are never uploaded anywhere, never shared, and never sold.

What we send to TikTok

Only content we made and chose to publish: a video file, its caption, and the privacy setting for the post. Nothing else is transmitted.

What we read from TikTok

The display name of the connected account, the privacy options that account allows, and a list of that account's own recent posts. The list is used for one purpose: to avoid publishing the same video twice. It is not stored beyond the moment of that check.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer any data to third parties. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking in this software.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and are deleted when the connection is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings, which immediately and permanently ends this software's access.

Children

This software is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than Soaring Games LLC.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date.
